# UA Player 2.0.2

UA Player 2.0.2 repairs subtitle playback and the LAMPA header after the full
player-core update in 2.0.1.

## Changes

- fixed a Media3 renderer-state conflict that could leave a selected primary
  subtitle track highlighted but invisible;
- kept the secondary subtitle renderer available while the primary subtitle
  line is switched off and restored both lines in the correct selection order;
- restored the consolidated subtitle layout so cue output is not left
  visually blank after renderer, resize, or second-line state changes;
- added live manual title search with posters, media type,
  season and episode catalogues, and manual number entry;
- preserved the LAMPA series title separately from per-episode labels, so a
  subtitle search starts with the series name instead of `Серія 5`;
- retained a manually selected series identity across adjacent playlist
  episodes and aligned their catalogue episode numbers;
- shortened the calculated finish label to a guaranteed single-line form such
  as `до 18:04`, preventing the time from being clipped on phones and Google TV;
- recognized Google TV through its Leanback/television features even when an
  external launch reports a phone-like UI mode, removing the empty rotation
  tile and restoring the TV remote-focus control row;
- retained UA Player branding, LAMPA/LampaUA integration, Watch Together,
  playlists, skip segments, session recovery, and Ukrainian as the only target
  for automatic subtitle translation.

Package: `com.lampaua.player`

Version: `2.0.2` (`versionCode 22`)
